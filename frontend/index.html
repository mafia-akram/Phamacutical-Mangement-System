DBMS Relational Architecture & Normalization

Formal verification of BCNF relations, Functional Dependencies, and Integrity Guards.

Decomposed Relations (BCNF)

  • Category (CategoryID, CategoryName)
  • Manufacturer (ManufacturerID, ManufacturerName, Country)
  • Medicine (MedicineID, Name, CategoryID*, ManufacturerID*, PricingCategory, RequiresPrescription, ReorderLevel)
  • Salt (SaltID, SaltName)
  • MedicineSalt (MedicineID*, SaltID*, Strength)
  • Supplier (SupplierID, SupplierName, Phone, Address)
  • Batch (BatchID, MedicineID*, SupplierID*, ExpiryDate, QuantityInStock, PurchasePrice)
  • PurchaseOrder (POID, SupplierID*, OrderDate, Status)
  • PurchaseOrderItem (POID*, MedicineID*, Quantity, Cost)
  • Customer (CustomerID, CustomerName, Phone)
  • Prescription (PrescriptionID, CustomerID*, DoctorName, PrescriptionDate)
  • Staff (StaffID, StaffName, Role, Username, PasswordHash)
  • Invoice (InvoiceID, CustomerID*, StaffID*, PrescriptionID*, InvoiceDate, TotalAmount)
  • InvoiceItem (InvoiceID*, BatchID*, Quantity, UnitPriceAtSale)
  • PriceHistory (PriceID, MedicineID*, MRP, SellingPrice, EffectiveFrom, EffectiveTo, ChangedBy*, Source)
  • SupplierQuote (QuoteID, SupplierID*, MedicineID*, QuotedPrice, QuoteDate)
  • MarketReference (RefID, MedicineID*, AvgMarketPrice, Source, RecordedDate)

ACID Constraints & Business Integrity

Lossless Join & Dependency Preservation: All decompositions preserve original functional dependencies through shared candidate keys with ON DELETE RESTRICT referential integrity.
  • Expiry Guard: Triggers reject inserts of batch with ExpiryDate < CURRENT_DATE.
  • Price Ceiling: Prevents price gouging above DRAP notified MRP.
  • Non-Negative Stock: Prevents phantom inventory through check constraints.
  • Controlled Dispensing: Prescription ID verified before selling schedule narcotics.
  • Temporal Price Versioning: Automatic timestamping of price revisions.
